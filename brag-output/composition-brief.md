2. Scene 2 (6s – 15s): Phone device mockup featuring live digital menu cards with realistic dishes, prices, and tags.
3. Scene 3 (15s – 24s): Interactive dish addition, comanda summary, and direct WhatsApp bubble transition.
4. Scene 4 (24s – 32s): Dual-card showcase: "Retiro en local (sin filas)" with live preparation status and "Consumo en mesa" with table QR.
5. Scene 5 (32s – 40s): Grand finale featuring animated PideTirúa logo, glowing ring, verified badges, "Pide • Disfruta • Apoya Local", and URL call to action.
